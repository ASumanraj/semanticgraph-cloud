SERVICES AGREEMENT
This Services Agreement is made and entered into as of January 10, 2021 by and between Alpha Corp and Zeta Inc.
Effective Date: January 15, 2021. Expiration Date: January 15, 2024.
Renewal: This agreement shall renew for successive periods of one year unless terminated upon thirty (30) days notice.
Termination: Alpha Corp reserves the right to cancel this arrangement without cause upon thirty days written notice.
Governing Law: This agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York.
Limitation of Liability: total liability shall not exceed one million dollars ($1,000,000).
Assignment: neither party may assign this Agreement without consent.
Restrictive Covenants: neither party shall compete during the term.
