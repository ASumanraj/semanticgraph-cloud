# T-223 · Candidate list for approval (sources and profiles, not individuals)

**Status:** for the founder's approval. **Nothing here has been contacted, scraped or messaged.** Outreach starts
only after the founder approves a named list; each name is added by the founder from their own network or from a
public speaker page they have looked at, one personal message at a time (guide, section 3).

## How this list is built

Named strangers are not listed. A list of private individuals pulled from the web is the scraping the guide rules
out, and a cold name is the weakest lead. Instead, each segment has (1) a warm-network ask, which comes first,
(2) public venues where the right people gather, and (3) the profile to look for. The founder picks the people.

Evidence labels: **verified** = read on the cited page; **unverified** = reasonable lead, not checked.

  to EU customers with many open-ended contracts; and data-practice associates at law firms advising them.
- **Warm network first:** ask the founder for any former colleague, customer, investor or advisor who works in legal
  operations or commercial contracts at a software, cloud or industrial company with EU customers. Ask each
  interviewee for two names.
- **Public venue (verified):** IAPP Europe Data Protection Congress, Brussels, 18-19 Nov 2026 (training 16-17 Nov).
  Useful for finding people through the public speaker and agenda pages, and for attending if the founder chooses.
  Speakers there are mostly privacy and data-governance staff, which also fits Segment B.
- **Unverified leads:** legal-operations and contract-management professional associations; LinkedIn groups on
  the Data Act; law-firm Data Act webinars (the firms publishing Data Act contract guidance, such as the PwC and
  law-firm pieces found in research, run client sessions).

## Segment B: sub-processor mapping

- **Profile:** DPOs and privacy counsel at software companies that publish a sub-processor list and a DPA.
- **Warm network first:** same ask as A.
- **Public venue (verified):** the IAPP Europe Congress above; the DPO Centre listing for it shows a community of
  practising DPOs.

## Segment C: amended credit agreements

## Revision 2026-10-07: opt-in channels only

The founder has no warm contacts in Segment A and will not supply names; no names are invented and no one is scraped.
The plan is now:

1. Identify opt-in channels for each segment: professional associations, special-interest groups, community forums,
   and organizers of public webinars on the topic. The founder checks each channel's rules and picks the ones.
2. Ask the organizer to share the invitation (`t223-invitation-drafts.md`, A and B). Respondents opt in by replying.
3. Interviews are remote. Referrals from interviewees are optional.
4. Replies and any contact details are kept in a private file outside the repository
   (`docs/research/private/` and `*.contacts.*` are git-ignored as a safeguard).
5. Brussels congress: not committed; treated as an optional later channel, not part of the first round.

Trade-off, stated plainly: opt-in replies come from people already interested in the topic, which biases toward
problems that are felt. The decision rules ask for specific past events, hours and budget owners, not interest, and
the guide's bias traps still apply. If fewer than five people reply in a segment after a few weeks, we report
that as a finding, not as a reason to lower the thresholds.
