AMENDED AND RESTATED SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Amended and Restated Supply Agreement (the “Supply Agreement”) is effective on the date of the last signature hereto (“Effective Date”), by and between ASH STEVENS INC., a Michigan corporation with its principal place of business at 5861 John C. Cerus desires to purchase Products for use or for inclusion in systems for resale by Cerus and/or its affiliates, distributors and licensees (the “Systems”).
