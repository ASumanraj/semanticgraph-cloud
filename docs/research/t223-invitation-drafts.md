# T-223 · Invitation and organizer-ask drafts

**Status: DRAFT. Not sent, not published.** Nothing below goes to anyone until the founder has reviewed the exact
text and named the recipient and approved. No names or contact details are kept in this repository.

## Rules

- Opt-in only: the organizer decides whether to share; the reader decides whether to reply.
- Say what it is, who is asking, how long, what is kept, and that nothing is for sale. No claim that the product is
  "first" or better than anything.
- Remote, 25 minutes, role and organisation type only, no names, no documents.
- Replies are handled in a private file outside the repository.

## A. Ask to an organizer (association, community group, webinar host)

> Subject: Request to share a short research invitation with your members
>
> Hello,
>
> I am an independent founder researching how teams deal with a change that touches many agreements at once, for
> example the EU Data Act's September 2027 date for existing contracts. I am not selling anything and have no
> product to demonstrate.
>
> Would you be willing to share the short invitation below with your members, in whatever channel you use for
> this? If your policy does not allow it, I understand, and a one-line reply is plenty. I would not contact anyone
> directly.
>
> Thank you for your time.
> [Founder name, contact]

## B. Invitation to share (Segment A)

> **Researching how teams handle contract changes. 25 minutes, remote.**
>
> In the last twelve months, did you have to find every agreement affected by a change, such as a new rule, an
> amendment or a vendor change? If so, I would like to hear how it was done: what triggered it, how long it took,
> what you had to show, and what did not work.
>
> - 25 minutes by video call, at a time you choose.
> - Nothing is for sale and nothing is demonstrated.
> - I note only your role and the type of organisation. No names, no documents.
> - Taking part is optional, and so is anything I may ask at the end, such as whether a colleague might also be willing.
>
> To take part, reply to [contact] with "interested".

## C. Screener (sent only after someone replies)

1. In the last twelve months, did you have to find every agreement affected by a change?
2. Roughly how many agreements were in scope?
3. Is a remote call of 25 minutes possible in the next two weeks?

## D. Optional closing line in the interview

"If anyone else comes to mind who dealt with something similar, you are welcome to pass on my invitation. There is no
expectation."

## Segments B to D

Same text with the trigger changed in the first sentence (a sub-processor change; an amendment and restatement; a
customer demanding source citations). Draft only when the founder starts that segment.
