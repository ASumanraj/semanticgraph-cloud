MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 1, 2024 ("Effective Date"), by and between Acme Global Solutions LLC, a Delaware limited liability company with principal offices at 100 Enterprise Way, Suite 400, Wilmington, DE 19801 ("Vendor"), and Beta Technologies Inc., a Delaware corporation with offices at 500 Silicon Boulevard, San Jose, CA 95110 ("Customer").

1. SERVICES AND STATEMENTS OF WORK
Vendor shall provide cloud architecture and AI substrate integration services as specified in one or more Statements of Work executed by both parties.

2. PAYMENT TERMS
Customer shall pay all undisputed invoices within thirty (30) days of receipt. Late payments shall accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION
This Agreement commences on January 1, 2024 and continues until terminated. Either party may terminate for cause upon thirty (30) days written notice of material breach.

4. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflicts of law principles.
